Changes made for rcwebsim (this adaptation is also CC-BY-SA-3.0):
* Exported from the original .blend to glTF by scripts/extra300/export.py:
  levelled out of its ground attitude, lamps, empties, the ground disc and
  the cockpit screens with missing images removed, meshes split by material,
  materials rebuilt as Principled BSDF.
* The three-blade propeller is now two-blade, like the RC model's.
* The Blender logos are painted out of the blue skin
  (scripts/extra300/delogo.py, using the author's PSD template).
* scripts/split-model.ts makes the propeller and control surfaces separate
  nodes hinged for animation; the elevator is split into halves.
