# Project context: agreed scope, current state, open work

Read when planning new work or judging whether something is in scope. The design was settled with the owner in a structured interview. Decisions that are hard to reverse are in `docs/adr/`. This file records the rest of the agreed scope.

## Agreed scope (v1)

| Area              | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Fidelity          | High: the plane should fly like a real RC model, not an arcade game. Prefer modelling the physical cause over adding a fudge factor.                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Aircraft          | The FMS 1700 mm PA-18 Super Cub (an electric high-wing trainer, taildragger, with flaps), drawn with helijah's CC-BY model, and the E-flite Extra 300 3D 1.3m (a neutral aerobat), drawn with Martins Upitis's CC-BY-SA model. Every model must look realistic: a plane without one waits for its model rather than ship with a placeholder. |
| Field             | One club field, about 2 km across: mown runway, pilot station, fence, tree lines that collide, gentle hills.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Platform          | Desktop browsers first; phones and tablets fly with on-screen touch sticks. Target 60 fps on a mid-range laptop's integrated GPU.                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Cameras           | Pilot view (default, with auto-zoom) and chase view. Walk mode moves the pilot round the field by keyboard; touch walking is not built.                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Input             | Keyboard, touch sticks, and the Gamepad API for gamepads and for the pilot's own radio on a USB sim cable. The radio is the transmitter and the sim its receiver (ADR-0012).                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Transmitter       | Mode 2 default (1–4 selectable), dual rates, expo, trims, throttle hold, 3-position flap switch, optional flap→elevator mix. Saved per aircraft in `localStorage`.                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Flight controller | An empty pass-through stage now: both aircraft fly as with their AS3X/SAFE switched off. Stabilisation is planned for later.                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Wind              | Steady wind plus Dryden turbulence. Wind shear, tree turbulence and thermals are planned for later.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Crashes           | Detect, show a message, reset by key. Planned for later: break-apart, then cumulative damage.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Lighting          | A fixed sunny afternoon. The sun direction is one value (`SUN` in `render/sky.ts`) so a time-of-day setting can drive it later.                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| UI                | The hangar on every load: a sunny open home garage made over as an RC workshop, the chosen plane in the sun patch, aircraft cards over the scene, the last plane flown preselected, Enter to fly. Settings overlay plus HUD in flight, with Back to hangar.                                                                                  |
| Spawns            | Runway start (default), air start, and hand launch at a launch speed the pilot sets.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Recording         | Recorded for tests and download only. No playback UI in v1.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Audio             | Basic synthesised motor and prop sound.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Tooling           | npm, Vite, TS strict, ESLint and Prettier, Vitest, Husky pre-commit (lint-staged, typecheck, tests). Deploys to GitHub Pages from `main`.                                                                                                                                                                                                    |

The owner tends to accept well-argued recommendations. Bring a recommendation with each question rather than an open menu.

## Current state

- **Headless sim core.** All tests pass: aircraft validation, each aircraft's flight envelope (`src/sim/aircraft/<id>.envelope.test.ts` over the shared scenarios in `src/sim/test-pilots.ts`), recording and replay, plus the touch sticks in `src/input/`.
- **Hangar.** A real-time garage scene (`render/hangar/`): about 40 downloaded props (Poly Haven CC0 and Sketchfab CC-BY, logos painted out), sun through the door and side window, ambient occlusion and a captured room reflection. The plane idles its prop and sweeps its surfaces; the camera orbits the whole room. The last plane flown is preselected and remembered (`settings.aircraft`). Back to hangar in the settings footer returns to it. A cold first visit on 4G (9 Mbps) shows the plane in its lit room at about 3 s and every prop by about 9 s; the sky and garden outside follow at about 14 s, held back by the ez-tree chunk (3 MB of built-in textures).
- **Balance.** The pilot can move the CG in settings (side view, lift point, draggable CG). It is saved per aircraft and recorded in flights.
- **Real radios.** A transmitter on a USB sim cable flies as the transmitter, with the sim as its receiver (ADR-0012): radio setup by moving each control, switches on axes, a channel monitor and a receiver failsafe. "Airframe needs trimming" (Balance tab) builds the plane off true so there is trimming to practise. Not yet tried with a real cable.
- **Browser app** deployed at https://rcwebsim.umari.dev/. It is an installable PWA and plays offline after the first load.
- **Animated models.** The prop spins (blades, then a blur disc), and the control surfaces follow their servos. `scripts/split-model.ts <id>` makes each split model from its export in `models-src/`. The Extra 300's export comes from the author's `.blend` through `scripts/extra300/`.
- **Hand launch.** Thrown from where the pilot stands at a set launch speed (default 11 m/s). Hands off at full power a firm throw climbs away and a 6 m/s one flies into the ground. The motor starts from rest at release, so it loses about 0.3 s of spool-up a real pilot would already have.
- **PA-18 tuning.** Hands-off glide about 12.5 m/s. 1 g clean stall 7.4 m/s. Top speed about 26 m/s. Static thrust about 3.2 kg on a full pack. Roll rate about 125°/s.
- **Extra 300 tuning.** Hands-off glide 13.6 m/s at 4.2:1. 1 g stall 8.0 m/s. Level on half throttle at about 18.6 m/s. Static thrust about 2.5 kg (ESC-limited), 1.6× its weight. Roll rate about 745°/s level at full power on high rates, matching flight video of the real model (705–830°/s); one axial roll in 0.9 s wanders 7° off heading. It holds a knife edge on about half its rudder with almost no roll or pitch coupling. Flaps don't exist on it, so the flap switch, mix and HUD indicator are hidden.

## Open work, roughly by value

1. **FMS manual citation.** The `FMS_MANUAL` source in `pa18.ts` is a truncated path. Find the full URL.
2. **Cruise test.** It holds altitude with an autopilot rather than solving for trim, and its bands are wide (cruise 14–23 m/s, roll 60–220°/s). Tighten them once there is better data.
3. **Gear behaviour while rolling.** Contact flickers when the plane is light on its wheels at speed. It's harmless so far, but watch it when tuning the ground run.
4. **Real-hardware checks.** Run `input-probe.html` with a FlySky FS-i6/i6X and its sim cable in Chrome on macOS, in normal and in Student mode. That settles whether the radio's trims reach the trainer port, and so whether `receiverOnly` can stay the default. Then fly radio setup with it, try a gamepad, and measure fps on a mid-range laptop.
5. **Servo transport delay** ([#1](https://github.com/hasauino/rcwebsim/issues/1)): model the dead time before a servo moves, and source real servo numbers.
6. **Extra 300 bands.** No published flight data exists for it. The roll rate is timed from flight video (airspeed unknown) and the knife-edge coupling bands rest on pilots' comments in those videos; the other bands rest on type-level physics (wing loading, L/D from CD0, drag power). Its aileron span is tuned in from the full-scale model's to hit the video roll rate, so the physics aileron starts further inboard than the model's. Tighten the bands from flight logs of the real model, especially airspeed in the rolls.
7. **PA-18 fuselage side force.** The Cub has no `sideForceSlope` body, so it knife-edges worse than a real one would. Adding it moves its yaw tuning.
8. **More aircraft.** The Edge 540, MXS and AeroScout wait for realistic 3D models; their issues hold the sourced data gathered so far.
9. **Hangar garden.** The garden outside the hangar waits on ez-tree's 3 MB chunk, so a cold first visit on 4G finishes at about 14 s against a 10 s target. Bake it into a small glb with WebP textures.
10. **Later features from the agreed list.** Stabiliser modes, time of day, wind shear and thermals, break-apart crashes, a second field, replay UI.
